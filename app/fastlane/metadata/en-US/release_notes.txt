First release.

Kyuhachi puts the whole 九州八十八湯 (Kyushu 88) challenge in one app: every eligible onsen, a map, and the record of your run at 88. Version 1.0 includes:

- Four challenges: by car, by public transport, by bicycle, on foot. Each with its own rules, bronze/silver/gold tiers, and the 見習い to 泉人 rank ladder.
- Visit logging with date, transport, duration, price, notes, a photo, and optional ratings for the water, atmosphere, cleanliness, sauna and rest area.
- The Spaport: a stamp for every onsen, filling in the order you bathe.
- The full onsen catalog with search, per-prefecture browsing, favourites, and a map.
- Imported GPS routes (.gpx, .kml, .tcx) with the onsens near them, plus Finder for shops, roadside stations, campsites and hotels ahead along the route.
- Area guides for the onsen regions of Kyushu.
- Stats: progress, timeline, geography, transport, budget and experience.
- Offline use: the catalog is on your device and visits recorded without signal sync later.
- English and Japanese.
